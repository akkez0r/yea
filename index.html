About Jesper.live

Jesper.live is a small personal gaming site where every game is built from scratch with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. There are no accounts, no installs and no tracking beyond what's needed to save your game progress locally in your own browser.

CS Case Clicker — how it works

You start with nothing but a clickable case. Every click earns you fake Euros. Spend those Euros in the shop on real CS2 cases — from the humble Recoil Case all the way up to the Operation Riptide Case. Each case you own permanently increases how much you earn per click, so your collection literally pays for itself. The game includes anti-autoclicker protection, so you'll have to earn your fortune with honest fingers. Your balance and inventory are saved in your browser, so you can close the tab and pick up where you left off.

Chess — how it works

The chess game implements the full rules of chess: legal move highlighting, castling, en passant captures, pawn promotion, and automatic check, checkmate and stalemate detection. You can play a local two-player match by passing the device back and forth, or switch to single-player mode and face a built-in AI opponent. A move history panel records the whole game in standard notation as you play.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to create an account?
No. Everything runs directly in your browser. Game progress (like your Case Clicker balance) is stored locally on your device using your browser's storage.
Does this work on mobile?
Yes — both games are fully responsive and playable on phones, tablets and desktops.
Is any of this real money or real CS2 items?
No. The Euros in CS Case Clicker are entirely fictional and the cases are just images. Nothing on this site involves real currency, gambling or real game items.
Will more games be added?
That's the plan! Jesper.live is an ongoing hobby project and new games get added when they're ready.
