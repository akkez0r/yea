Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 9, 2026

This privacy policy explains how Jesper.live ("we", "us", "this site") handles information when you visit and play games on this website.

Information we collect

Jesper.live does not require accounts, does not ask for personal information, and does not operate any backend servers that store your data. The games run entirely in your browser.

Local storage

Some games (for example CS Case Clicker) save your game progress — such as your in-game balance and inventory — using your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device and can be removed at any time by clearing your browser's site data for Jesper.live.

Cookies and advertising

This site uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info.

For more information on how Google uses data when you use our site, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Third-party content

Case images in CS Case Clicker are loaded from the Steam community content delivery network (steamstatic.com). Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. These third parties may receive standard technical information (such as your IP address) when their content is loaded.

Children's privacy

This site is a general-audience gaming site. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new "last updated" date.
